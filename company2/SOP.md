# 🛠️ Company 2: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## 🔄 The Intellectual Authority Workflow

### Phase 1: Knowledge Extraction (Kodari)
1. **Trigger**: New topic or mission.
2. **Action**: Scan `brain/manuscripts` for philosophical alignment.
3. **Action**: Search S-grade academic databases for scientific grounding.
4. **Output**: `Draft_v1` (Dual-Track content: Science + Philosophy).

### Phase 2: Visual Translation (Visual Master)
1. **Trigger**: `Draft_v1` completion.
2. **Action**: Select a classic masterpiece as the base.
3. **Action**: Generate Dual-Track visual prompts in **Soft Sage** style.
4. **Output**: Scientific Blueprints + Existential Art.

### Phase 3: Media Adaptation (Leo & Instagram)
1. **Trigger**: `Draft_v1` + Visuals.
2. **Action**: Convert into high-retention video scripts (Youtube) and engagement-focused news cards (Instagram).
3. **Output**: Multi-platform assets.

### Phase 4: Monetization & Community (Hyunbin & Youngsook)
1. **Trigger**: Asset distribution.
2. **Action**: Guide Lv.1-2 readers to Lv.3 community or Lv.4-5 solutions.
3. **Output**: Revenue & Brand Loyalty reports.

---
## 📏 Strict Rules
- No content without a cited source from Nature, Science, or equivalent (S-grade).
- Every visual must use the Soft Sage (#D4E2D4) palette.
- No "CEO planner" tasks can skip the Kodari verification phase.
